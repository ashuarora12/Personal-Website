Who Benefits? Health, Jobs & Gender in the World Bank Group Portfolio

An analysis of the World Bank Group's own FY25 results data: 1,131 projects in 125 countries. It asks who is being reached, whether the portfolio measures the link between health, jobs and labour mobility, and whether projects deliver to women as planned.

Methods include a rule-based text classifier with manual precision review, a cross-validated early-warning model for results falling behind schedule (with fairness checks by region and fragility), and propensity-score matching comparing fragile and non-fragile settings. Limitations and data provenance are documented on the page.

Interactive dashboard · Data science · 2026

What Drives Global Patenting Activity?

An econometric and machine-learning study of what predicts how many patents a country files, and which countries file more or fewer than expected. It matches WIPO resident patent filings from 141 countries with GDP, population and energy-use data.

The dashboard lets you compare countries and regions, explore a world map of over- and under-performers, and build a hypothetical country to see what the fitted model predicts. Methods include OLS, panel fixed effects (2,201 observations, 2000–2021), a Negative Binomial count model, and Gradient Boosting with cross-validation.
