Economist · Researcher

Ashu Arora

I am an economist and public policy researcher with a Master’s in International Economics and Finance from the Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies (SAIS). My research interests include international development, gender, migration and economic policy. I have previously worked at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad and contributed to research published in the Journal of Development Studies. I am currently working with Limon Rodriguez, Economist, World Bank, on research related to migration and health.
