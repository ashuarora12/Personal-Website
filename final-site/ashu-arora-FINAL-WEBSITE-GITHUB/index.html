About

Research, evidence and ideas.

I am an economist and researcher with a Master’s degree in International Economics and Finance from Johns Hopkins University’s School of Advanced International Studies (SAIS). My research interests lie at the intersection of economic development, labour markets, migration, skills, employment, and public policy.

I currently work with World Bank Economist Limon Bade Rodriguez on research related to labour migration, skills, and employment, including work on Global Skill Partnerships (GSPs), brain drain, skills development, and the international mobility of workers. My work involves analysing survey and administrative data, applying quantitative methods, and translating empirical evidence into insights on economic and policy questions.

Previously, I worked at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad, where I conducted research in economics and co-authored research published in The Journal of Development Studies. My broader research experience spans development, labour markets, inequality, health, international capital mobility, and macroeconomic policy.

Beyond research, I am interested in intellectual community-building, communication, and programme development. As a Head Fellow at the International Student House in Washington, DC, I organized international dialogues and policy events and founded the Waldo Stevenson Forum.

I am particularly interested in using data, economic research, and clear communication to understand complex development challenges and contribute to evidence-based policy solutions.
