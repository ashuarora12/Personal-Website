Global Skill Partnerships: Migration, Skills & Employment

Research Assistant — World Bank · Ongoing

With Limon Bade Rodriguez. Work includes a quantitative impact evaluation of a Global Skill Partnership pilot in the ICT sector involving Morocco and Belgium.

Survey and administrative data; treatment/comparison groups; Propensity Score Matching; covariate balance; robustness diagnostics; employment and job-search outcomes.

Do the Feldstein-Horioka Results Hold Post-COVID?

MA Capstone — Johns Hopkins SAIS · Ongoing

Examines the relationship between domestic saving and investment using OECD countries from 1980–2023, with IMF WEO, OECD and Our World in Data.

Methods: Panel Fixed Effects · OLS · Stata · Excel
