Projects

Things I want to build

These projects turn research questions and public data into accessible tools for exploring evidence, policy and economic change.

India Development Observatory

Interactive exploration of income, poverty, employment, health, education, gender, inequality, urbanisation and state-level development.

AI & the Indian Labour Market

Exploring AI exposure across occupations, workers, industries, regions and skill levels.

Key distinction: AI exposure is not the same as job displacement.

India Policy Evidence Explorer

Policy → Intervention → Study → Method → Outcome → Evidence. A searchable evidence base preserving methodology and limitations.

Policy Autopsy

Exploring policies that failed, were reversed or produced unintended consequences. Why do policies fail even when the underlying idea appears sound?

Data Gaps Atlas

Exploring what we do not know about important development questions, including missing data and measurement gaps.

AI Research Benchmark

Testing how well AI systems perform tasks involved in empirical research, from reading papers to interpreting results and identifying threats to identification.
